Hold FX — Punch

  • fx+a key: an effect on the whole mix while the key is held. Never recorded.

Hold EDIT — Erase
  • Playing: edit+key erases that sound or note from every step the playhead passes while you hold it — MPC style. Stopped: from the whole pattern at once.
  • K1 SHIFT every step later / earlier · K2 LENGTH ×2 / ½ · K3 TRANSPOSE the synth notes.
  • edit+black key on the drum track: MUTE mode — the white keys mute sounds instead of erasing them.

4. Recording

What REC Does
  • Playing: records the selected track now. rec again stops recording; the loop plays on. Every pass is an overdub.
  • Stopped, with notes: arms (rec ready). Your first note is step 1.
  • Stopped, empty project: a free take.
  • Turn ALGORITHM mid-take: the take hops tracks.
The Free Take
  • Play freely: no click, no count-in. Press rec on the "1" after your last bar.
  • SLOOP picks 1, 2 or 4 bars, sets the tempo from your playing, snaps the notes and loops at once. All four tracks take that length.
  • play drops the take. 24 s max.
REC Screen, While Armed
  • K1 mode free / tempo · K2 length 1, 2, 4 bars · K3 start note / count-in.
Click, Swing, Quantise
  • Audible click: GLO → GLOBAL → CLICK OFF / REC / ON. Never recorded.
  • Swing 0–100; 100 is 75/25. Global on TRACKS K1; per track seq+K3 adds on top.
  • Notes snap to the track's DIV; for looser, DIV 1/32 or nudge. POLY sounds record chords of up to 4.
Undo & Clear
  • edit+oct− undo · edit+oct+ redo. One level only.
  • Hold rec ~2 s clears the selected track. A ring fills; let go early and nothing happens.

Levels, Not Velocity

The DRUMS Screen

2. Anatomy & the Big Ideas

SLOOP is free, unofficial firmware (isod89, on Felucca): the FM-1 as a four-track loop groovebox. The panel labels no longer tell the truth — this sheet does.

Four Tracks
  • ALGORITHM picks the track you play, record and edit. 1 blue · 2 green · 3 yellow are synths sharing 8 voices; 4 orange is drums, 6 voices of its own.
  • PRESETS is that track's sound (or kit). SELECT is tempo, or page back / forward.
  • On screen, white = what you are touching, red = recording.
Tap vs Hold — Every Button Has Two Lives
  • Tap opens its pages (sound design, settings). Tap again for the next page.
  • Hold opens a layer: the 16 white keys become 16 tiles and K1–4 new dials. Let go, back to playing. Layers: fx edit arp seq sel glo save. ENV and LFO only have pages.
  • Lock a layer open: hold it and tap home. Any other button lets go.
  • In a layer keys 1, 5, 9, 13 glow dimly; SELECT stays tempo.
Notation
  • Key 1–16 = the white keys, F3 to G5. Key 5 is C4. "First four black keys" = F#3 G#3 A#3 C#4.
  • GO values (SAVE, LOAD, NEW, ERASE…): one click shows AGAIN:, a second within 1.5 s does it.
Housekeeping
  • home tap: TRACKS, again: visualiser. Hold: menu (lights, colours, USB audio, version). oct−+oct+: octave 0.

6. Steps, Locks & Rolls

Hold SEQ — Steps
  • White keys are the 16 steps of the page; lit ones play. An empty step is set with the drum sound shown, or the last note or chord you played. Tap a set step to clear it.
  • Page 1–16 … 49–64: first four black keys, or oct− / oct+.
  • K1 sound / note · K2 DIV 1/32–2BAR · K3 swing · K4 LENGTH 1–64. Per track, so polymeters are free.
SEQ + Hold a Step + …
  • K1 drums: which sound · synths: the note
  • K2 drums: level · synths: velocity 1–127
  • K3 ratchet x1–x4 · K4 nudge −32…+31, in 64ths of a step
  • oct+ fill condition: normal → FILL ONLY (F) → NO FILL (×)
  • oct− clears nudge, locks and condition
  • A step you turned a knob on stays set on release.
Parameter Locks
  • 1. Turn the parameter on its page first — that becomes the lock target. 2. Hold seq + the step. 3. Turn PRESETS. ALGORITHM picks another target.
  • 24 locks a track. A dot on the tile means nudged or locked. Undo does not bring locks back.
STEP Page & ARP Rolls
  • Tap seq on a synth track: the keys write the cursor step, acid style. K3 TIE / REST, or LEN on a note · K4 ACC / SLD. Tap edit to clear a step.
  • Hold arp + a key: note repeat on the grid. K1 rate 1/8 to 1/64. Recorded as ratchets. Tap arp for the real arpeggiator.

7. Key, Chords & Mix

Hold SEL — Key
  • sel+any key sets the key of the song for all three synth tracks.
  • K1 CHORD OFF, TRIAD, 7TH, 9TH, SUS4, POWER · K2 SCALE, 16 of them · K3 KEYS OFF / SNAP / WHITE · K4 transpose ±24.
  • With a chord on, the white keys play the scale's chords: C4 is the I, D4 the ii… One finger, one chord, always in key.
CHORD+ — Hold a Black Key
  • F# major ↔ minor · G# +7th · A# sus4 · C# +9th · D# inversion. Stack them: F#+G# turns G into Gm7.
  • Tap sel twice for SEL 2: STRUM 1–60 ms and VLEAD voice leading.
  • Black keys silent? A chord or KEYS is on. Set both OFF.
Hold GLO — Mix
  • Keys 1–4 mute · 5–8 solo · 9 fill while held · 10 fill the next bar · 16 tap tempo · F#3 MIDI LEARN.
  • K1–K4 track levels · SELECT tempo.
  • Mutes fade in milliseconds and the pattern runs on in time.
TRACKS Screen & Master
  • Tap home: K1 swing · K2 level · K3 steps · K4 pan, for the selected track.
  • GLO → TEXTURE: a vinyl, tape, rain, wind, radio or room bed under the mix, without spending a track.
  • Per track: FX page sends (drive, chorus, delay, reverb), SLICER, and an INSERT — clip, fold, fuzz, crush, phaser, flanger, chorus.

8. Song, Save & MIDI

Hold SAVE — Song
  • Keys 5–8 save the loop as section A–D. Keys 1–4 play A–D from the next bar. Tap several while held for a chain, up to 8.
  • 13 loop / song mode · 14 SONG REC: what you play is written as the song · 16 the SONG screen.
  • Sections A–D are project slots 1–4. Autosave keeps your work once stopped and idle for 2.5 s.
MIDI, Sync, Audio
  • In over USB and the 3.5mm TRS jack. No Bluetooth. Out over USB: GLO → SYSTEM → OUT SEQ sends the patterns.
  • Channels 1, 2, 3 play tracks 1–3; drums on 10. GLO → MIDI to change.
  • Clock: GLO → SYSTEM → SYNC INT / USB / TRS.
  • On USB it is also an audio input, Felucca, recording the master.
Rescue
  • oct− at power-on: USB rescue · oct−+oct+: calibration. Back up in the web editor before every update.
