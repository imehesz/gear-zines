5. Play & Record

Live Record
  • Count-in: tap codex, then fabula. Four beats, then it rolls.
  • No count-in: press codex + fabula together.
  • From the pattern start: codex, then altero + fabula.
  • Punch out: codex stops recording, keeps the beat rolling. fabula stops all.
Pattern Length
  • Defaults to 1 bar (ln.1). Up to 99 bars per group.
  • Set it: codex, then − / +. Hold codex to change it on the fly.
  • Double it: codex, then hold altero + +.
Step Sequence
  • Stopped, − / + walk step by step.
  • Write a note: hold codex + paed.
  • Velocity: hold altero, turn X. Duration: Y.
  • Nudge: hold altero + paed, tap − / + (ticks in free time, steps when quantized).
Claves (Keys)
  • claves spreads one paed chromatically across all 12.
  • Octave: hold claves + − / +. Transpose: hold claves + another paed.
Erado & Undo
  • Hold erado + paed while playing to rub out notes as they pass.
  • Hold until TRK blinks = wipe that paed's whole track.
  • erado + group paeds until PTN = erase pattern. erado + manus until SCN = erase scene.
  • Undo: altero + grp B.

4. Sonus & Sonus Edito

Picking Sounds (sonus)
  • Press sonus, hit a paed, then − / + to browse. 200+ sounds on board.
  • altero + − / + jumps ten at a time.
  • Know the number? Hold sonus and type it on the paeds.
  • X level   Y pitch.
Sonus Edito — altero + sonus

Six pages, stepped through with − / +:

  • sonus — X play mode (oneshot / key / legato), Y pan.
  • trim — X start point, Y length.
  • envelope — X attack, Y release.
  • caerbannog — time stretch. X picks BPM or BAR mode, Y sets the sample's own tempo or length so it locks to the project.
  • midi — X channel, Y root note. Turns a paed into an external instrument.
  • muto group — only one sound in the group sounds at a time. Everything else cuts off.
Copy, Paste, Undo
  • altero + grp C copies, altero + grp D pastes, altero + grp B undoes.
  • In manus: once grabs the bar, twice grabs the whole pattern.
  • In sonus: copies the sound. Press twice for every sound in the group. Pick the target paed before pasting.
  • Bin a user sample: hold erado + sonus until SND blinks.

Muto group is what makes hi-hats behave — put the closed and open hat on the same muto group and the closed one chokes the open one, like a real pedal.

3. Workflow & Manus

The Hierarchy

9 projects. Each holds 4 groups (A–D), each group 99 patterns, arranged into up to 99 scenes.

Getting Around (manus)
  • manus is home; the screen shows the current step.
  • Hold manus — pick a scene, 01 to 99.
  • Hold manus + paeds 1–9 — switch project.
  • Hold a group + − / + — pick that group's pattern.
  • altero + grp A — jump to the next empty pattern.
Committo — altero + manus
  • Stamps the current arrangement as a scene, then hands you a fresh one built on top of it.
  • How you build structure: get a loop right, committo, tweak, committo again.
  • Doubles as a duplicate when you want a variation.
The Faedr
  • Out of the box it rides group level.
  • Reassign: hold faedr, pick a target from the labels above the paeds.
  • Hold a group paed and move it to set that group's volume.
  • Record the move: hold codex and sweep it.
  • Fine control: hold altero while moving. Reset: altero + faedr.
  • Wipe all automation: stopped, hold erado + faedr 2 seconds.

Treat the groups as A drums, B bass, C melody, D loops & foley. The whole machine assumes it.

6. Sampling, Chop & Time

Sample Mode — altero + demus
  • Hold a paed to record from the built-in mic or line in. It lights when it catches something.
  • − / + pick the input: LR stereo, L, R, or C mono centre.
  • X and Y set the amp / record level.
  • From a phone or laptop: in sample mode, press + until the source reads usb, then hit a paed.
  • Leave with manus.
Chop — altero + demus + demus
  • Auto-chop: pick a group, − / + sets the slice count. It follows the beat.
  • Live chop: tap paeds during playback to drop cut points by hand.
  • Then X / Y nudge each slice's start and end.
Quantum
  • X note interval — 8, 16 or 32 per bar, plus 8T (12) and 16T (24) for triplets. Y swing.
  • − / + flips between quantized and free-time recording.
  • Note repeat: hold quantum + paed for rolls at the current interval.
  • Quantum correctus (altero + quantum): tidy one paed, leave the rest. Stopped: press a paed to straighten its steps. Playing: hold it to fix only what goes by.
Necto — altero + tempus
  • A tempo-locked loop trap. X slides it, Y sets its length.
  • Out with tempus (instantly) or manus (on the next bar).

WAVs move over USB-C with the EP sample tool: teenage.engineering/apps/ep-sample-tool

7. Pocus (the FX)

Press pocus, sweep the faedr to send a group in, and use − / + to change which effect it lands on.

The Rack
  • Dungeon Echo (delay) — X echo time, Y feedback.
  • Torture Chamber (reverb) — X length, from small room to cathedral. Y colour, dark to bright.
  • Distortion — X drive, Y colour. A digital axe.
  • Bardic Ensemble (chorus) — X depth, Y speed.
  • Filter — X cutoff: left cuts the highs, right cuts the lows. Y resonance.
  • Compressor — X drive, Y speed.
Punch-In Pocus
  • Hold pocus and press the group paeds mid-performance for instant mangling.
  • They are pressure sensitive — lean in harder for more.
  • They stack. Hold two at once.
  • Let go and it snaps straight back to dry.
Live Input Pocus
  • From pocus, press manus to bring the live input into the rack.
  • X input volume, Y how much goes to the effects.
Summa — altero + pocus
  • Master compressor across the whole mix. X drive, Y speed.

Run a Pocket Operator, a guitar or a voice through the line in and the torture chamber becomes an outboard reverb.

8. Systema & Sync

Settings — altero + erado
  • Walk with − / +, confirm with intra, and leave with manus.
  • Velocity: paed settings → vel → off, hi (light touch) or low (heavy touch).
  • Scale: 12 of them, for claves mode.
  • Metronome: sequencer settings → on (rec + play), rec, or cnt (count-in only). Then press tempus and turn Y up.
Tempus
  • Tap tempus repeatedly to tap in a tempo.
  • X sets BPM (40–399), or hold tempus and type it on the paeds.
  • Time signature: manus + tempus, then X / Y.
Sync Recipes
  • Two Medievals: 3.5 mm from sync out to sync in, both set to 1/16.
  • Drive a PO: PO on SY2, Medieval sync out set to 8. Start the PO, then hit fabula.
  • Follow a PO: PO on SY1 via a stereo-to-dual-mono cable, Medieval sync in set to 8.
  • Old drum machine: sync in set to 24.
  • MIDI keyboard: TRS-A cable, detected automatically. Press claves to spread one paed across the keys.
