5. Play & Write

Playing
  • Hold sound + 1–16 to choose a slot, then press 1–16 to play it.
  • Nothing audible? Press write once — you are in write mode.
Write Mode
  • write enters and exits. Press 1–16 to put sounds or notes into the grid. Lit steps are active; press a lit step to remove it.
  • A pattern is 16 steps.
  • play to hear it back.
Live Record
  • While playing, hold write and punch 1–16. Notes quantize to the current swing setting. Release write when finished.
Tempo & Volume
  • bpm cycles HIP HOP 80, DISCO 120, TENCHO 140.
  • Fine tune: hold bpm + turn B. Range 60–240.
  • Swing: hold bpm + turn A.
  • Volume: hold bpm + 1–16. Treat 5 as the headphone ceiling.

Swing lives on the bpm button, and live record quantizes to it — so set the swing before you punch anything in, not after.

4. Tone, Filter, Trim

Six parameters across three pages, both knobs on each. Press FX to step between the pages.

The Pages
  • TONE — A pitch, B volume.
  • FILTER — A high-/low-pass, B resonance.
  • TRIM — A start point, B length.
Trim Is Where the Work Is
  • Trim is how you turn a sloppy grab into a usable hit. Cut the silence off the front and the tail off the back.
  • In the drum section each slice trims individually, and the slice you edit is the last one you triggered.
  • So: tap the slice, then turn the knob. Tap a different slice and the knobs are now editing that one instead.
Parameter Locking
  • While a pattern plays, hold write and turn A / B to lock the currently selected parameters into the pattern.
  • Only tone and filter are lockable. Trim is not.
  • Locks apply to the currently selected sound.

Filter sweeps locked over eight bars are the entire PO-33 sound. Lock one, then chain the pattern four times.

3. Sampling

Recording
  • Hold record + a key 1–16. The mic records for as long as you hold both, and the take lands in that slot.
  • Plug a cable into line in and it samples from that instead of the mic. The jack decides.
  • The LCD counts down the seconds you have left out of 40.
  • Memory full? Delete something or record straight over an existing slot.
Choosing the Half
  • Record into a melodic slot to play the sample as a pitched instrument.
  • Record into a drum slot to spread it across the keys as slices.
  • You cannot convert one to the other later. Decide before you hold record.
Managing Sounds
  • Delete: hold record + sound bins the currently selected sound.
  • Copy a sound: hold write + sound, press 1–16 for the destination.
  • Copy a single slice: hold write + sound, press a drum key 9–16, then any key 1–16. Copies the last slice you triggered.

40 seconds goes fast. Sample short and trim hard — a half-second vocal chop earns its place, four bars of a song does not.

2. Anatomy & Lexicon

The Buttons
  • sound hold + 1–16 picks the sound slot
  • pattern hold + 1–16 picks the pattern
  • bpm tap for tempo; hold for volume, swing, fine tune
  • write toggles rec mode — the rec symbol lights
  • play start / stop
  • FX effects, and the three knob pages
  • record sampling — also delete, clear and sync
  • A B the two parameter knobs
Melodic and Drum — the Big Idea
  • 16 sound slots split into two halves, and a sample behaves differently depending on which half you recorded it into.
  • Melodic section: keys 1–16 are a scale, and each key triggers the whole sound.
  • Drum section: each key is a slice of the sound.
  • Same recording, two instruments. Sample a bar of a break into both and you get a bassline and a chopped kit.
Numbers
  • 40 seconds of sample memory, shared across all slots. Seconds remaining show on the LCD while recording.
  • 16 patterns of 16 steps. Chains up to 128 — the longest in the range.
  • Two AAA. Sleeps to a clock; battery status is sound + bpm.

6. Patterns, Songs & Data

Patterns
  • Hold pattern + 1–16 to select. Blinking LED is the active one.
  • Copy: hold write + pattern, press 1–16 to paste there.
  • Clear: record + pattern.
Chaining a Song
  • Hold pattern and tap patterns in order. Up to 128.
  • Repeats allowed: 1,1,1,4 plays 1 three times, then 4, then loops.
Backup & Transfer
  • Transmit: write + sound + play.
  • Receive: write + sound + record.
  • Unit to unit: 3.5mm stereo, line out to line in. Receiver goes into receive mode first.
  • To tape: record the transmission on any stereo device, then play it back into line in with the unit receiving.
  • Receiving erases every sample and pattern on the target the moment the transfer starts. Verify on a second unit if the original matters.
Lock Tab
  • Snapping it freezes all patterns — and disables sampling for good.

7. Effects

While playing, hold FX + 1–15. With write mode on the effect is saved into the pattern. To wipe saved effects, hold FX + 16 in write mode.

  • 1. loop 16
  • 2. loop 12
  • 3. loop short
  • 4. loop shorter
  • 5. unison
  • 6. unison low
  • 7. octave up
  • 8. octave down
  • 9. stutter 4
  • 10. stutter 3
  • 11. scratch
  • 12. scratch fast
  • 13. 6 / 8 quantize
  • 14. retrigger pattern
  • 15. reverse
  • 16. no effect
How to Use Them
  • 1–4 are a loop ladder. Walk down 1→4 and the loop tightens into a stutter — the classic build.
  • 11 / 12 scratch is the one people buy this thing for. Hold it over a vocal chop.
  • 13 6/8 quantize flips a straight beat to triplets live.
  • 16 is no effect — it is the off switch, and in write mode it is the eraser.

8. Sync & Setup

Sync Modes

Stereo cable between units. The signal splits: audio on the right, click on the left. Default is SY0.

  • SY0 in stereo → out stereo
  • SY1 in stereo → out mono/sync
  • SY2 in sync → out stereo
  • SY3 in sync → out mono/sync
  • SY4 in mono/sync → out stereo
  • SY5 in mono/sync → out mono/sync
Syncing
  • Hold record + press bpm on the master to cycle modes.
  • Press play on the follower first — it waits for the clock. Then play on the master.
  • Chaining three POs: master SY1 → middle SY5 → last SY4.
  • Sync levels must not exceed 5Vpp.
Clock & Reset
  • Set time: A hours, B minutes, any key confirms.
  • Alarm: sound + pattern, then 1–16 for the pattern it wakes you with.
  • Factory reset: hold pattern + write while inserting batteries.
