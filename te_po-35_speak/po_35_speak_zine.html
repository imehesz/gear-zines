5. Play & Write

Making a Sound Speak
  • Hold sound + 1–16 to choose the slot. Then press 1–16 to play it up the keyboard.
  • Silence? Press write once. You were in write mode and did not know it.
Write Mode
  • write enters and exits. The rec symbol on the LCD is the only tell.
  • Press 1–16 to lay notes into the grid. Lit steps are active; press a lit step to take it out.
  • A pattern is 16 steps, always.
  • play to hear it.
Live Record
  • While playing, hold write and punch 1–16. Notes quantize to the current swing setting. Let go of write when done.
Parameter Locking
  • While a pattern plays, hold write and turn A / B to record knob moves into the pattern.
  • It locks whichever page FX currently has selected, and applies to the selected sound.

Lock a formant sweep across four bars and one sampled syllable turns into an entire vocal line.

4. The Eight Voices

Hold FX + 1–8. With write mode on, the voice change is baked into the pattern, so a phrase can change character mid-bar.

  • 1. neutral
  • 2. autotune
  • 3. retro
  • 4. noise
  • 5. robot
  • 6. fifth
  • 7. vocoder
  • 8. synth
The Knob Pages

Press FX to toggle which pair the knobs hold. Four parameters, two pages:

  • pitch A / formant B — pitch moves the note, formant moves the throat. Pull them apart and a whisper becomes an ogre or a wasp.
  • start A / speed B — where in the sample playback begins, and how fast it runs through.
Transpose & Scale
  • Hold pattern + turn A — change key.
  • Hold pattern + turn B — change scale.

Autotune plus a scale is the whole cheat code — set the scale, and every key you hit is in tune with the tonic channel whether you sang it right or not.

3. Getting Your Voice In

Sampling
  • Hold record + a key 1–15. The mic records for exactly as long as you hold both; the take lands in that slot.
  • 8 seconds per slot — it is a per-slot budget, not a shared pool.
  • Plug a cable into line in and it samples from that instead of the mic. The jack decides; there is no menu.
  • To replace a take, just record over the slot.
What Records Well
  • Short and loud. One syllable stretches further across 16 keys than a whole word does.
  • Record flat and close. The formant knob is doing the character work later — performing it into the mic just fights the voice model.
  • Consonants survive pitching down; sibilance does not survive pitching up.
Slot 16 — the Drum Machine
  • Hold sound + 16 for the built-in PO-32 tonic channel.
  • It cannot be sampled over. Build the beat there, the voices everywhere else.

The mic is on the front. Cup your hand behind the unit and talk into it from 5cm — it gets you level without clipping.

2. Anatomy & Lexicon

The Buttons
  • sound hold + 1–16 picks the sound slot
  • pattern hold + 1–16 picks the pattern
  • bpm tap for tempo; hold for volume, swing, fine tune
  • write toggles rec mode — the rec symbol lights
  • play start / stop
  • FX voices, effects, and the knob pages
  • record sampling, clearing, receiving
  • A B the two parameter knobs
What It Actually Is
  • A vocal synth. Sample your own voice, then play it back pitched and formant-shifted across the 16 keys.
  • 15 voice slots. Slot 16 is a single-channel PO-32 tonic — the only drums on board.
  • 8 seconds per slot. 16 patterns of 16 steps. Chains up to 64.
  • Sounds are shaped by 8 voices, not by an oscillator. Pick the voice first, tune second.
Housekeeping
  • Two AAA. Auto-powers-off to a clock — press any key to wake it.
  • Battery status: sound + bpm.
  • Factory reset: hold pattern + write while inserting the batteries.
  • Snapping the lock tab freezes patterns and sounds — and kills sampling permanently.

6. Patterns, Songs & Data

Patterns
  • Hold pattern + 1–16 to select. The blinking LED is the active one.
  • Copy: hold write + pattern, press 1–16 to paste into that slot.
  • Clear: record + pattern wipes the active pattern.
Chaining a Song
  • Hold pattern and tap the patterns in the order you want them. Up to 64.
  • Repeats are allowed: 1,1,1,4 plays pattern 1 three times, then 4, then loops.
Backup & Transfer
  • Transmit: write + sound. Receive: record + sound.
  • Unit to unit: 3.5mm stereo cable, out to in. Put the receiver in receive mode first.
  • To tape: record the transmission to any stereo device at 16-bit / 44kHz or better, then play it back into line in with the unit in receive mode.
  • Receiving erases everything on the target the moment the transfer starts.
Microtonic
  • Sonic Charge Microtonic (standalone or VST/AU) can replace the tonic drum sound over mic or line in. Put the PO in receive mode, hit the destination button in the plug-in.

7. Effects, Tempo & Clock

While playing, hold FX + 9–15. With write mode on the effect is saved into the pattern; to wipe saved effects, hold FX + 16 in write mode.

  • 9. stutter sweep
  • 10. trance gate
  • 11. half rate
  • 12. 16 bar build up
  • 13. 6/8 quantize
  • 14. retrigger pattern
  • 15. reverse
  • 16. no effect
Tempo
  • bpm cycles three presets: HIP HOP 80, DISCO 120, TENCHO 140.
  • Fine tune: hold bpm + turn B. Range 60–240.
  • Swing: hold bpm + turn A.
Volume
  • Hold bpm + 1–16.
  • On headphones, treat 5 as the ceiling. It goes far past comfortable.
Clock & Alarm
  • Set time: A hours, B minutes, any key confirms. The only way to reset it is pulling the batteries.
  • Alarm: sound + pattern, set with A / B, then 1–16 picks the pattern it wakes you with. Any key stops it; A fully down disables it.

Effect 12, the 16 bar build up, is a whole arrangement in one button — punch it in on the last 16 bars before a drop and let it do the work.

8. Sync & Setup

Sync Modes

Stereo cable between units. The signal splits: audio on the right, click on the left. Default is SY0.

  • SY0 in stereo → out stereo
  • SY1 in stereo → out mono/sync
  • SY2 in sync → out stereo
  • SY3 in sync → out mono/sync
  • SY4 in mono/sync → out stereo
  • SY5 in mono/sync → out mono/sync
Syncing
  • Hold record + press bpm on the master to cycle modes. The mode shows top-right.
  • Press play on the follower first — it waits for the clock. Then play on the master.
  • Chaining three POs: master SY1 → middle SY5 → last SY4.
  • Following a volca, phone or laptop: PO on SY2 or SY3.
  • Sync levels must not exceed 5Vpp.
