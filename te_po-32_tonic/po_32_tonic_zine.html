5. Effects

While playing, hold FX + 1–16. To record them, enter rec mode with write first. To clear, hold FX in rec mode without pressing anything.

  • 1. half rate
  • 2. distortion
  • 3. squash
  • 4. echo fade
  • 5. pitch lfo
  • 6. eq sweep
  • 7. mega morph
  • 8. pitch bend up
  • 9. punch

  • 11. beat-repeat
  • 12. beat-repeat faster
  • 13. fm
  • 14. granular
  • 15. reverse

The Ones Worth Knowing
  • 11 / 12 beat-repeat and its faster twin are the fill button. Tap between them for a stutter roll.
  • 10 6/8 quantize shoves a straight beat into triplet feel without rewriting it.
  • 3 squash is the pumping compressor sound. It glues the whole kit.
  • 16 bouncing ball is an accelerating retrigger — an ending in one press.

Effects only stick if rec mode was lit when you pressed them. If a punch-in vanished on the next loop, that is why.

4. Making a Beat

The Loop
  • Hold sound + 1–16 to pick a sound.
  • Press write. Check the rec symbol is lit.
  • Press 1–16 to place hits. Lit steps are active.
  • play to hear it. Pick the next sound and go again.
Accent
  • In play mode: acc + 1–16 accentuates as you press.
  • In rec mode: acc + 1–16 programs accented steps into the pattern.
  • Accent is what stops a 16-step grid sounding like a metronome. Use it before you reach for swing.
Parameter Locking
  • Hold write and turn the knobs to write parameter changes over time.
  • In rec mode, hold a single step and turn the knobs to set parameters for that step only.
  • Per-step pitch on a tom is a fill. Per-step morph on a snare is a whole drummer.
Live Record
  • While playing, hold write and punch 1–16. Quantized to the current swing. Release when done.

Shaping Them
  • A is pitch, B is morph — the same two parameters Microtonic exposes.
  • Every sound is synthesised, not sampled, so pitching does not degrade it. Drag 13 bass down and it is still clean.
Solo & Mute
  • Solo: hold sound, then press and hold 1–16.
  • Mute: while playing and not in write mode, hold any key 1–16 to mute that channel. Several at once is fine.
  • Muting is your only mixer. It is how you get an intro and a breakdown out of one pattern.

2. Anatomy & Lexicon

The Buttons
  • sound hold + 1–16 picks the sound
  • pattern hold + 1–16 picks the pattern
  • bpm tap for tempo; hold for volume, swing, fine tune
  • write toggles rec mode — the rec symbol lights
  • play start / stop
  • FX the 16 effects
  • acc accent — also clear, receive and sync
  • A B pitch and morph
Four Channels, Sixteen Sounds
  • The grid's columns 1–4 are the four channels. Sounds 1–4 live on channel 1, 5–8 on channel 2, and so on.
  • That is why hi-hat pairs and kick variants sit where they do — sounds on the same channel cut each other off.
  • 16 patterns of 16 steps. Chains up to 64.
Power
  • Two AAA. Sleeps after 5 minutes idle (60 with a cable in line in), keeping everything, LCD only. Any key wakes it.
  • Battery: sound + bpm. Reset: hold pattern + write while inserting batteries.
  • Clock: A hours, B minutes. Alarm: sound + pattern, then 1–16 for the pattern.

6. Songs & Tempo

Patterns
  • Hold pattern + 1–16 to select. Blinking LED is the active one.
  • Copy: hold write + pattern, press 1–16 to paste there.
  • Clear: acc + pattern.
Chaining a Song
  • Hold pattern and tap the patterns in order. Up to 64.
  • Repeats allowed: 1,1,1,4 plays 1 three times, then 4, then loops.
Tempo & Volume
  • bpm cycles HIP HOP 80, DISCO 120, TENCHO 140.
  • Fine tune: hold bpm + turn B. Range 60–240.
  • Swing: hold bpm + turn A.
  • Volume: hold bpm + 1–16. Treat 5 as the headphone ceiling.
Lock Tab
  • Snapping it write-protects every pattern permanently. Pull the batteries to return to the locked state; solder the two pads to undo it.

7. Microtonic & Data

Replacing Sounds
  • Sonic Charge Microtonic — standalone or VST/AU in any DAW — can overwrite any of the 16 sounds.
  • Dial the sound in the plug-in. A on the PO maps to pitch, B to morph, so set those two where you want them to land.
  • Press the TE logo in the plug-in to prepare the transfer.
  • On the PO: acc + sound to enter receive mode, then hit the destination button in Microtonic.
  • Over the air: hold the PO's mic near the computer speaker. Or run a 3.5mm cable into line in.
Backup & Transfer
  • Transmit: write + sound. Receive: acc + sound.
  • Unit to unit works over the built-in speaker and mic, or line out to line in. Put the receiver in receive mode first.
  • To tape: start any recorder, then transmit. Restore by playing it back into a unit in receive mode.

The speaker-and-mic transfer is the party trick: two PO-32s facing each other on a table will swap a whole kit with no cable at all.

8. Sync

Sync Modes

Stereo cable between units. The signal splits: audio on the right, click on the left. Default is SY0.

  • SY0 in stereo → out stereo
  • SY1 in stereo → out mono/sync
  • SY2 in sync → out stereo
  • SY3 in sync → out mono/sync
  • SY4 in mono/sync → out stereo
  • SY5 in mono/sync → out mono/sync
Syncing
  • Hold acc + press bpm on the master to cycle modes.
  • Press play on the follower first, then play on the master.
  • Chaining three POs: master SY1 → middle SY5 → last SY4.
  • Following a volca, phone or laptop: PO on SY2 or SY3.
  • Sync levels must not exceed 5Vpp.
