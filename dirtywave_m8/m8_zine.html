5. Instruments

Seven Types
  • WAVSYNTH wavetable and noise · MACROSYN over 40 models, ported from Mutable Braids · SAMPLER · FMSYNTH 4-op, 12 algorithms · HYPERSYNTH 6-note stereo chords · MIDI OUT with 10 user CCs · EXTERNAL INST, MIDI out with the audio coming back through M8's filters and sends.
  • NONE is not a bug — it is how you and the M8 both know a slot is free.
  • 128 instruments per song, each with its own table, plus 128 more tables you can assign freely.
Getting Around
  • opt+←→ previous / next instrument · opt+↑↓ jumps ±16.
  • edit+play previews the instrument.
  • shift+opt copies the whole instrument, shift+edit pastes it — and pressing it again undoes the paste.
  • shift+↑ is the modulation view; again is the Instrument Pool, all 128 slots at once.
Modulation
  • Four slots per instrument: AHD, ADSR, DRUM, LFO, TRIG (fired by another track), TRACKING (note or velocity).
  • ADSR only reaches release if the phrase has an OFF note or command. Without one it sustains forever.
  • Set DEST to MOD AMT / RATE / BOTH and a slot modulates its neighbour: 1→2, 2→3, 3→4, 4→1.

Highlight any instrument parameter, then shift+← or shift+→ to the phrase or table — the default FX command is now that parameter. It is how you look up the command for anything.

4. Playing & Live

Play Means Different Things
  • play plays what you are looking at: the song from the cursor in song view, otherwise just that chain, phrase or instrument.
  • shift+play plays all tracks from the song cursor no matter which view you are in. This is the one you want.
Mutes & Solos — Anywhere
  • opt+shift mutes the current track. opt+play solos it.
  • Both are momentary unless you release OPTION first — that latches them.
  • opt+shift+play clears every mute and solo.
  • These work in every view, not just the mixer.
Song View
  • opt+←→ solos everything to that side of the cursor.
  • ←+play cues the selected row while running.
  • opt+↑↓ jumps 16 rows.
  • opt held with playback stopped shows the track's running time.
  • opt opt opt sets or clears a bookmark on a chain.
Live Mode
  • shift+← from song view enters and exits it. Tracks start, cue and stop independently from anywhere in the song.
  • A cued track waits for the playing chain to end. Change that under LIVE QUANTIZE in the project view.
  • Loading a song while one plays queues it to the end of the chain — that is the set-change.
File Browser
  • ← top of list, → bottom, opt+↑↓ skips 8, play previews, shift+opt sorts, opt+edit deletes, opt exits.

3. The Grid Language

Song, chain, phrase and table are all grids, and they all take the same handful of commands. Learn these once and the whole machine opens.

Values
  • edit on an empty cell “--” inserts the last value you edited or deleted.
  • edit+↑↓ coarse, edit+←→ fine. On a note column that is octaves and semitones.
  • edit+opt cuts the value, or resets a parameter to its default.
  • edit edit double-tap creates a new unused chain, phrase, instrument or table on that column.
Selection
  • shift+opt enters selection mode. Move the cursor to grow the block.
  • opt copies and exits · edit+opt cuts · shift+edit pastes.
  • edit+↑↓ inside a selection shifts the contents up or down.
  • One column, several rows, then shift+edit — interpolates between the top and bottom values. Ramps for free.
Clone — the Important One
  • shift+opt, then edit copies what is under the cursor into a new number.
  • On song view, shift+opt, then edit edit deep-clones: the chain and every phrase inside it.

A star on the title (PHRASE 00*) means that phrase plays somewhere else too. Edit it and you edit every one of them. Clone it first.

2. Anatomy & Lexicon

Four Keys and a Cross
  • edit is also YES / enter. opt is also NO / exit a sub-view.
  • shift on its own does nothing at all.
  • Notation: a+b means hold a, press b. a, then b means press and release a, then press b.
How a Song Is Built
  • 8 tracks, each monophonic — one note at a time, each with its own play position.
  • A track plays a vertical list of chains. A chain is up to 16 phrases plus a transpose column. A phrase is 16 steps of note, volume, instrument and three FX columns.
  • An empty phrase cell ends the chain; an empty chain cell loops the track to its top. Chains of different lengths drift apart on purpose — use an empty chain to hold a silent track in step.
  • 256 song rows. Park experiments on the unused ones underneath.
Views
  • shift+direction moves between views. The mini-map at the bottom right shows where you are.
  • Left to right: song → chain → phrase → instrument → table.
  • Above: project over song, groove over phrase, modulation then instrument pool over instrument. Below song: mixer, then send effects.
Hex
  • Everything counts 0–9 then A–F. 40 is 64. FF is 255.
  • Two digits often mean two things: ARP37 is +3 semitones then +7. The help line at the bottom of the screen translates while you edit.

6. Sampling

Samples Stream Off the Card
  • Nothing loads into memory, so there is no length limit — but the card's random read speed is the whole ballgame. Use a card off dirtywave.com/sd.
  • CPU TOO BUSY is almost always the card. Three cures: mono where stereo earns nothing (kicks), 16- or 8-bit, and stop playing samples an octave or more above their recorded pitch — each octave doubles the data rate. 1- or 2-channel PCM .wav only.
Recording
  • Instrument TYPE = SAMPLER, then edit on REC. (EDIT if a sample is loaded). opt leaves.
  • Start with edit on START — or with the power button, which keeps the key click out of a mic take.
  • SRC picks line, mic (Model:02), USB or TR1–8; ARM starts on a threshold. Name and SAVE before leaving the view.
Editing
  • SELECT sets the range; opt+↑↓ snaps the marker to the tempo in beats.
  • PROCESS, then edit again to run it: crop, delete, duplicate, normalize, silence, reverse, invert, fade, xfade loop, mono, downsample, 16- or 8-bit, SLICE:AUTO by transient, or even divisions.
  • Lazy chop: with the song playing, tap edit on the slice row to drop a marker at the play-head.
  • 128 markers, saved inside the wav. With SLICE set to 01 FILE they map up from C-1; SLI cues them.

Song view, select a block, then double-tap edit: Selection to Sample renders it into a new instrument.

7. FX Commands

Three columns per step, in phrases and in tables. Hold edit and press ↑↓ on a command column for the help browser — pick with the direction keys, release edit to insert it.

  • ARP arpeggio
  • CHA chance
  • DEL delay row
  • GRV groove
  • GGR groove, all
  • HOP jump row
  • INS change instr.
  • KIL kill note
  • NTH every nth loop
  • OFF note off
  • PBN pitch bend
  • PSL portamento
  • PVB vibrato
  • RET retrigger
  • REP repeat cmd
  • RTO repeat limit
  • RND randomise
  • RNL rand. left
  • SCA track scale
  • SED random seed
  • SNG song hop
  • TBL table
  • TBX aux table
  • TIC table speed
  • TPO tempo
  • TSP transpose
  • VOL volume
  • PIT pitch
  • DJC dj filter
  • VT1–VT8 trk vol
The Ones With Teeth
  • CHA1F gives the note about a 1-in-16 chance and leaves the other commands at 100%. X is everything left of the command, Y everything right.
  • REP is sticky: it keeps incrementing across rows and phrases until a new command or REP00 stops it. A ^^ in the lane means one is still running from an earlier phrase.
  • HOPFF stops that track dead.
  • DJC sweeps the global DJ filter: 80 is off, below it low-pass, above it high-pass.
  • NTH skips by loop count — the fill that only lands every fourth time round.

8. Mixer, Project & Setup

Mixer
  • OUTPUT VOL returns to F0 at every power-up whatever you left it at — deliberate ear protection, not a fault.
  • shift+opt snapshots the entire song; shift+edit recalls it. A free undo before you wreck something live.
  • opt+touchscreen assigns the touch axis to the highlighted parameter; opt+a MIDI CC maps that CC.
Project
  • Tap edit rhythmically on TEMPO for tap tempo. TRANSPOSE, GROOVE and SCALE land when you release the edit key — dial mid-bar, drop on the beat.
  • CLEAR UNUSED frees every chain, phrase, instrument and table the song does not use. RENDER writes 44.1kHz wavs to /Renders, mixed or as stems.
  • Samples are not stored in the project file. Move one and the song loads without it. BUNDLE gathers all three into /Bundles — do that before tidying the card.
Hardware
  • 3.5mm TRS Type A MIDI in and out. Stereo line in, through the effects. USB carries power, class-compliant audio, MIDI and the virtual display.
  • Power button: 1 second on, 2 off, 7 to force off if it locks up.
