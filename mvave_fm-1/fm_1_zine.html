5. ENV & LFO

ENV — Not a DX7 Control
  • Knobs 1–4 are Attack, Decay, Sustain, Release, each 0–8, drawn as blocks. 0 attack is instant, 8 release is a long fade.
  • A DX7 voice has no ADSR at all — its whole shape lives in the six operator envelopes. This layer is the FM-1's own, sitting over the top, and the manual does not say how the two combine.
  • So a note that will not die has two suspects: Release here, and Rate 4 / Level 4 on the carriers over in edit.
LFO — Page 1
  • Wave Triangle, Saw Down, Saw Up, Square, Sine, Sample & Hold.
  • Speed 0–99 · PMD 0–99, pitch depth · AMD 0–99, amplitude depth.
LFO — Page 2
  • Delay 0–99 is the fade-in before the LFO arrives. It is what makes vibrato sound played rather than switched on.
  • Pitch Sens 0–7 · Lfo Sync 0 free, 1 restarts on each note · Osc Sync 0 free-running, 1 resets oscillator phase per note.
Traps
  • On a DX7, PMD and Pitch Sens multiply. Expect the same here: PMD at 99 with Pitch Sens at 0 gives no vibrato whatsoever. Set Pitch Sens first, then reach for PMD.
  • Osc Sync also lives in edit → GLO. One voice parameter, two doors.
  • Speed is a bare number, not a note division. Nothing in the LFO follows tempo.

4. The Effect Chain

Six Slots
  • fx opens a 3×2 grid: Filter, Reverb, Delay, Distortion, Chorus, Phaser. Bright box = on, dim = off, blinking dot = selected. fx again returns Home.
  • SELECT moves the dot between slots — in this mode only, it does not change page.
  • Knob 4 is the on/off for every effect, clockwise on, counter-clockwise off. It is never a sound control, so each effect only ever has three parameters — and Filter and Reverb spend one of those on a Type.
Knobs 1 · 2 · 3
  • FILTER Type LP/BP/HP · CutOff 100Hz–20kHz, 100 log steps · Q 0–100
  • REVERB Type Room/Hall/Plate · Decay · Mix — both 0–100% in 10% steps
  • DELAY Decay · Rate · Mix
  • DISTORTION Gain · Tone · Level
  • CHORUS Freq · Depth · Mix
  • PHASER Freq · Depth · Mix
  • All unmarked ranges are 0–100%. The bottom bar shows the real value — CutOff: 1004Hz, Mix: 70%.
Reordering — SEL
  • sel does nothing outside FX mode.
  • Press sel to grab the selected effect: the dot stops blinking and goes solid, SEL LED on.
  • Turn SELECT — the grabbed effect swaps with its neighbour and walks along the chain.
  • Press sel again to drop it there. LED off.

The chain runs 1 to 6 in slot order. Distortion after Reverb is a different machine from Distortion before it — sel is the only way there.

3. Presets, Banks & Saving

128 Voices, No Bank Button
  • PRESETS scrolls 001–128 and wraps both ways. Every click loads immediately — you hear it as you turn.
  • A SysEx import writes a 32-voice bank into a slot you choose, A / B / C / D. Four slots of 32 is exactly the 128, so an imported bank lands in one quarter of the list and you reach it by scrolling to it. There is no bank key and no categories.
  • The top bar always shows the loaded number and name.
Saving
  • save is press and hold. A tap does nothing. It commits voice edits, effect settings, sequencer patterns and global settings; the LED lights and the screen says Preset Saved.
  • glo → page 2 → Knob 2 is Globe Save, which writes the global settings to flash on their own.
  • Panel Transpose is the exception — the manual says it saves to global settings by itself.
  • Factory reset: glo → page 2 → Knob 1 clockwise, then Knob 4 confirms, Knob 1 cancels. It restores all 128 voices, so any imported banks go with them.
Three Things Called Transpose
  • oct− oct+ — ±3 octaves. Global, not part of the voice.
  • oct+ALGORITHM — ±12 semitones. Global, auto-saved.
  • edit → GLO → Transpose 0–48 — this one is inside the voice, the DX7's own key transpose, and 24 is centre.
  • Chasing a tuning problem means checking all three. oct−+oct+ zeroes the first two at a stroke.

Nothing on this machine has an undo. Park experiments on a preset you are willing to lose.

2. Anatomy & Notation

One Pot, Seven Encoders, Twelve Buttons
  • MASTER is the only potentiometer. The other seven — SELECT PRESETS ALGORITHM and KNOB 1–4 — are endless: nothing has a position, and the screen is the only truth.
  • Top row: fx sel env lfo edit glo. Bottom row: home save arp seq play/stop rec. Every one toggles — press it again, or home, for the oscilloscope.
SELECT Is the Page Key
  • Most modes have two pages. Clockwise is page 2, back is page 1; the bottom bar reads 1/2 Arpeggio.
  • Two exceptions: in FX it walks the six effect slots; in EDIT it pages the selected operator, up to six deep.
  • Knobs 1–4 do whatever the bottom bar says, and that changes with every mode and every page.
Octave, Transpose, Reset
  • oct− oct+ shift one octave per press, to ±3. The LED lights on the side you have gone: off = 0, slow blink ±1, fast blink ±2, solid ±3.
  • Hold oct− or oct+ and turn ALGORITHM for transpose, 0 to 12 semitones; the held button picks the direction.
  • oct−+oct+ resets octave and transpose to 0. The one combo worth memorising.
The Two Long Presses
  • home held toggles Bluetooth — blinking = on but not connected, solid = connected, off = off. save held writes to flash.

ALGORITHM is live — one click rewires the voice you are holding, with no undo and no confirmation.

6. EDIT — the DX7 Underneath

Press edit and the labelled keys become the section picker: OP1–OP6, PIT, GLO, MONO, POLY. Selectable keys blink, the chosen one goes solid. SELECT pages, Knobs 1–4 edit, edit leaves.

Six Pages Per Operator
  • 1 Rate 1–4 (0–99) — envelope rates
  • 2 Level 1–4 (0–99) — envelope levels
  • 3 Detune 0–14 · FreqCoarse 0–31 · FreqFine 0–99 · OscMode Ratio/Fixed
  • 4 A ModSens 0–3 · KeyVelocity 0–7 · Out Level 0–99
  • 5 L Depth · R Depth (0–99) · BreakPoint A-1 to C7
  • 6 L Curve · R Curve (−LN/−EX/+LN/+EX) · RateScale 0–7
  • PIT page 1 Rate 1–4, page 2 Level 1–4 — the pitch envelope.
  • GLO Algorithm 0–31 · Feedback 0–7 · Osc Sync · Transpose 0–48.
Traps
  • The same 32 algorithms are numbered twice: 01–32 on the ALGORITHM encoder, 0–31 in EDIT → GLO. Subtract one.
  • Detune 0–14 is the DX7's −7…+7. Centre is 7, not 0.
  • There is no operator on/off switch. Mute one by taking its Out Level to 0.
  • Out Level is volume on a carrier and brightness on a modulator — which one depends on the algorithm.
  • Feedback belongs to whichever operator the current algorithm loops back — change algorithm and it moves.

Every parameter above is a Yamaha DX7 voice parameter renamed. Forty years of DX7 programming guides apply here one for one, and any DX7 bank loads over SysEx.

7. ARP & SEQ

Never both at once: with SEQ running arp answers "Disable SEQ to enter", and with ARP running seq answers "Disable ARP to enter". Turn the running one off first.

ARP
  • Page 1 Mode · Octave 1–4 · Rate · Tempo 30–300 BPM
  • Page 2 Gate 20–100% · Swing 50–75% · Latch
  • Modes: Up · Down · Inclusive bouncing, endpoints included · Exclusive bouncing without repeating them · Random · Order, as you pressed them · Repeat, each note repeated before moving on.
  • Latch on holds the pattern after you let go; press new keys to replace it, Latch off to stop.
SEQ
  • Press seq: SEQ and STOP light, and the keys become 16 step lamps.
  • rec starts recording (REC + PLAY lit). Play notes — each lands on the next step. rec again drops you into playback.
  • play/stop toggles the transport. Step LEDs: solid filled, dark empty, blinking playhead.
  • Page 1 Pattern 1–16 · Clear · Rate · Tempo. Page 2 Gate · Swing. 16 patterns, held in flash.
  • Clear is Knob 2, right beside Pattern on Knob 1, and it wipes whichever pattern is selected. Read the number first.
  • play/stop or rec with SEQ off only says "Enable SEQ first".
Traps
  • Tempo is a knob, 30–300. The manual documents no MIDI clock input — plan on the FM-1 running to its own clock, not a DAW's.
  • Entry is sequential only. Nothing documents jumping to a step or entering a rest. Gate and Swing are per-pattern, not per-step.

8. Globe, MIDI & Jacks

GLO
  • Page 1 Note Channel 1–16 · Effect Channel 1–16 · Pitch Bend Up 1–24 · Down 1–24, set separately. Page 2 Reset Patches (K1) · Globe Save (K2).
MIDI
  • Three inputs are live at once: USB-C, BLE (long-press home), and 3.5mm MIDI IN through a 3.5mm-to-DIN adapter.
  • There is no MIDI OUT jack. Keyboard, arpeggiator and sequencer notes leave over USB and Bluetooth only, on the Note Channel.
  • Receives note on/off with velocity, pitch bend, CC and SysEx dumps. The only CC channel the manual names is the Effect Channel — treat CC as an effects hook, not a synth-parameter one.
  • SysEx: send a 32-voice DX7 bank and a bank screen appears; Knobs 1–4 pick slot A/B/C/D.
  • Sounding note = key + 53 + (octave × 12) + transpose. At octave 0 the 27 keys cover F3 to G5.
Jacks & Power
  • Audio out is 3.5mm and wants a standard TRS plug. The manual warns other connectors can add noise and can fail to mute the speaker — on a TS cable, expect it to keep singing.
  • USB-C carries power and USB MIDI; the power switch is on the rear. 2000mAh gives about 12 hours, and LOW BATTERY holds the whole screen until you charge.
