5. Effects

While playing, hold style + 1–16 to punch in. Hold style with no key pressed to clear them.

  • 1. low sample rate

  • 7. hipass filter
  • 8. hipass sweep

  • 12. parameter lfo
  • 13. octave up
  • 14. octave down
  • 15. half note up

Which Ones Earn Their Keep
  • 5–8 the filters are the bread and butter — the sweeps are a build in a single button.
  • 13 / 14 octave up and down turn a bassline into a lead and back without touching a note.
  • 9 / 10 stutter 4 and 3 against each other make the fill you would otherwise have to program.
  • 11 feedback is the one to be careful with on headphones.

Effects are momentary and are not saved into the pattern here — if you want the move to stick, record the PO's output, not the pattern.

4. Play Styles

Hold key + 1–16 while playing to punch in chords, arpeggios and transpositions over whatever the sequencer is already doing.

Using Them
  • Release key when finished.
  • Hold key without pressing anything to clear the play styles back out.
  • They sit on top of the pattern, so one 16-step line can carry a whole progression without you rewriting a single step.
Parameter Locking
  • Hold write and turn A or B to write knob movement into the pattern over time.
Tempo & Volume
  • bpm cycles HIP HOP 80, DISCO 120, TECHNO 140.
  • Fine tune: hold bpm + turn B. Range 60–240.
  • Volume: hold bpm + 1–16. Treat 5 as the ceiling on headphones.

Write a flat one-note pulse across all 16 steps, then play the whole song with key alone. The PO-14 is happiest as a performance instrument.

3. Notes & the Grid

Building a Line
  • Hold sound + 1–16 to choose the sound.
  • Press write. Check the rec symbol is lit.
  • Press 1–16 to place notes. Lit steps are active; press a lit step to remove it.
  • play to hear it.
Pitch & Length — the Core Move
  • In write mode, hold a lit step and turn A to set that note's pitch.
  • Same hold, turn B to set its length.
  • This is per-step. It is how you get a bassline instead of sixteen identical thuds, and it is the single most-forgotten combo on the unit.
Micro Drum (sound 16)
  • Hold sound + 16 to switch to the drum machine.
  • In write mode, hold a step and turn A to choose which of the 16 drum sounds fires there. n1–n16 shows top-right.
Live Record
  • While playing, hold write and punch 1–16. Notes quantize. Release write to stop.

2. Anatomy & Lexicon

The Buttons
  • sound hold + 1–16 picks the sound
  • pattern hold + 1–16 picks the pattern
  • bpm tap for tempo; hold for volume and fine tune
  • write toggles rec mode — the rec symbol lights
  • play start / stop
  • style the effects button
  • key play styles — chords, arps, transpositions
  • A B the two parameter knobs
Two Buttons, Not One
  • This is the PO that trips people coming from the others. It has both style and key where a PO-32 or PO-33 has one FX.
  • style mangles the sound. key rearranges the notes.
  • key is also the one that clears patterns and toggles sync — jobs done by record or acc elsewhere.
What It Is
  • A bass synth. Sounds 1–15 are assorted bass synthesis — FM, phase distortion, wavetable, and physically modelled string.
  • Sound 16 is a micro drum machine with 16 sampled sounds.
  • 16 patterns of 16 steps. Chains up to 16 — the shortest chain in the range.

6. Patterns & Songs

Patterns
  • Hold pattern + 1–16 to select.
  • A pattern is 16 steps, always.
Copy
  • Hold write + pattern to copy the active pattern to memory.
  • Keep holding both and press 1–16 to paste into that slot. Letting go loses it.
Clear
  • key + pattern wipes the active pattern. Note it is key here, not style.
Chaining a Song
  • Hold pattern and tap patterns in order. Up to 16.
  • Repeats allowed: 1,1,1,4 plays pattern 1 three times, then 4, then loops from the top.
  • 16 is a tight budget. Spend it on structure, not on repeats you could get by writing a longer pattern.

The chain is the arrangement and there is no undo. Sketch the order on the back of this zine before you tap it in.

7. Housekeeping

Power
  • Two AAA. Watch the polarity.
  • Auto power-off after 5 minutes idle — patterns and settings are kept, only the LCD stays lit, and it draws almost nothing. No need to pull batteries.
  • With a cable in line in it waits 60 minutes instead.
  • Any key wakes it.
  • Battery status: sound + bpm.
Clock & Alarm
  • Set time: turn A for hours, B for minutes, any key confirms.
  • Alarm: sound + pattern, set with A / B, then 1–16 for the alarm sound. A all the way down disables it.
  • Resetting the clock means pulling the batteries and starting over.
Factory Reset
  • Hold pattern + write while inserting the batteries.
  • Older firmware does not have this. If nothing happens, that is why.

The 5-minute sleep is not off. If the PO seems dead but the clock is showing, it is just napping — press anything.

8. Sync

Sync Modes

Stereo cable between units. The signal splits: audio on the right, click on the left. Default is SY0.

  • SY0 in stereo → out stereo
  • SY1 in stereo → out mono/sync
  • SY2 in sync → out stereo
  • SY3 in sync → out mono/sync
  • SY4 in mono/sync → out stereo
  • SY5 in mono/sync → out mono/sync
Syncing
  • Hold key + press bpm on the master to cycle modes.
  • Press play on the follower first — it waits for the clock. Then play on the master.
  • Chaining three POs: master SY1 → middle SY5 → last SY4.
  • Following a volca, phone or laptop: PO on SY2 or SY3.
  • Sync levels must not exceed 5Vpp.
