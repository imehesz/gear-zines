5. Play & Record

The Pads
  • Banks A–D on the bank buttons; for E–H, shift + bank or double-press it. 8 × 16 = 128 pads.
  • full level pins every hit to 127; shift + it is half level (64). Hold it to set a custom user level.
  • 16 level copies the last pad across all 16 with one parameter ramping low→high — velocity, tune, filter, layer, attack, decay, probability or ratchet. Hold it + a pad to change which pad it clones.
  • note repeat held + a pad retriggers it. Rate follows the tempo and the TC setting. shift + note repeat latches it on.
Recording a Sequence
  • rec arms — and erases the existing events on that pass. overdub layers without erasing, and switches itself on after one full pass in record.
  • play from the playhead, play start from the top, and shift + play start for the metronome. stop twice silences notes still ringing; shift + stop returns to 1:1:0.
Fixing It
  • Hold erase + a pad while the sequence runs to rub that pad out as the playhead passes. Stopped, it opens the Erase window.
  • TC opens Timing Correct: Start nudges events onto the grid, End and Length only move the ends. shift + TC toggles it.

Hold shift while turning the data dial or a Q-Link for fine resolution — 0.01 on tempo and tuning.

4. Trim & Chop

sample edit opens it. The Trim/Chop button in the lower-left corner swaps the two. Trim crops the ends off one sample; Chop cuts one sample into slices.

The Audition Pads
  • 09 play all
  • 13 loop, held
  • 10 play S→E
  • 14 play to loop
  • 11 S→E, held
  • 15 play from loop
  • 12 —
  • 16 loop, latched
  • Pads 1–4 are one-shot from start / to start / to end / from end. Pads 5–8 are the same four, held.
Chopping
  • Four methods in the lower-left selector: Manual, Threshold (transient detection — higher value, more slices), Regions (equal divisions), BPM (bars, beats, time div).
  • Live slicing: press an unlit pad to start the sample, then press unlit pads during playback to drop markers. Green is the newest marker, yellow are existing ones. Pressing a lit pad restarts from that slice instead.
  • Slice+ drops a marker at the cue playhead.
  • shift + 0 Snap forces edges onto zero-crossings. This is the cure for clicks.
  • Link Slices keeps slices edge to edge. Turn it off to make overlapping or non-contiguous slices.
  • shift + Clear All wipes every marker.

shift + Convert → New drum track using slices builds a kit whose pads point at the slices — no new sample files. Tick Create Events and it writes a sequence playing them in order.

3. Sampling

sampler opens Sampler mode.

Recording
  • Set the source in the top-left menu: Input 1,2 for the rear jacks, or Resample to record the MPC's own output.
  • Level with the rear rec vol knob. The meter must not sit at the top.
  • Drag the threshold slider low (around −50 dB) and tap Arm. Recording begins when the input crosses it — or tap the round record button to start by hand.
  • Tap stop and the Keep or Discard window appears: Name, Track, Assign To Pad, Root Note.
  • Keep puts it in the project. Save writes it to disk. Edit jumps straight into Sample Edit.
The Other Two Ways In
  • Looper — shift + sampler. Record and overdub audio in real time, then export the loop as a sample.
  • Auto Sampler — on the Sampler page. Plays a chromatic run at an external synth or a plugin and captures every note into a keygroup automatically.
Sample Processing
  • Process in Sample Edit: pitch shift, time stretch, normalize, reverse, fade, gain, silence, and the rest.
  • Pitch Shift transposes without changing the length; Time Stretch changes the length without changing the pitch.

Keep is not Save. Keeping a sample only puts it in the project in RAM — power down without saving the project and the take is gone.

6. Tracks, Sequences & Saving

Six Kinds of Track
  • Drum — samples on pads. Keygroup — samples mapped chromatically, up to 128 keygroups of up to eight samples.
  • Audio — recorded audio or a resample. Plugin, MIDI (out to hardware), CV (out to modular).
Arranging
  • main is home: sequence, track, bars, tempo.
  • next seq turns the pads into sequence triggers, queued to the end of the bar — the song structure becomes a live performance.
  • track mute and shift + track mute for pad mutes. Both take mute groups, and both quantize to the TC setting.
  • Grid View (shift + main): pencil draws notes, eraser removes them. shift + tap for Select All, Copy, Cut, Paste, Duplicate, Delete. Nudge moves by the TC value; hold Don't Snap to move by 4 ticks.
Saving — Read This One
  • shift + browse saves the project, samples and all.
  • Auto Save is off until you turn it on: Menu → Preferences → Project Load/Save → Auto Save Enabled, plus a timeout. Do it once, on a new machine, before anything else.
  • Browser: shift + Load All loads a whole folder; shift + a folder button bookmarks where you are; shift + Delete File removes one.
  • Export: Main mode → the Project field → Export. Set an Audio Tail of a couple of seconds or reverbs get guillotined.

7. Q-Links & Performing

Q-Link Knobs
  • Four knobs, but sixteen parameters: they address one column at a time. q-link steps to the next column, shift + q-link to the previous. The lights above the knobs say which column is live.
  • They are touch-sensitive — resting a finger on one shows its parameter without moving it.
  • shift + sample edit is Q-Link Edit, where you assign what the columns control.
  • In Trim and Chop, column 1 is the start point and column 2 the end point, coarse at the top and fine at the bottom.
Pad Perform
  • shift + 16 level. Lays a scale or chords across the pads: Chromatic, Notes, Chords, Chromatic Chords, in a chosen key.
  • It works on keygroup, plugin, MIDI and CV tracks. On a drum track it does nothing — the mode opens, the pads ignore it.
XYFX and Automation
  • shift + next seq turns the touchscreen into an XY effect pad, two parameters on two axes. Your finger moves are recorded as automation.
  • shift + step seq flips global automation between Read and Write. Left in Write, every knob you touch is being recorded.
Step Sequencer
  • step seq makes the 16 pads into 16 steps for the selected pad — drum-machine entry with no timing to get wrong.

8. Ports & Setup

Round the Back
  • Inputs 1–2 and Outputs 1–2, balanced ¼″ TRS. One stereo pair each — that is all of them.
  • MIDI In and Out, 5-pin DIN.
  • 4 × CV/Gate on ⅛″ TS. One signal per jack, or two per jack with a TRS-to-dual-mono breakout (Hosa YMM-261) — eight in total.
  • USB-B to a computer for MIDI and audio. USB-A for a flash drive or a MIDI controller.
  • Link — Ethernet for Ableton Link. MPC One only; the One+ dropped it. Link also works over Wi-Fi.
  • Front: SD card slot and ⅛″ headphones.
  • Power: One is 19 V / 3.42 A, One+ is 12 V / 3 A, both centre-positive. Not interchangeable.
Cards & Files
  • exFAT is the recommended format. FAT32, NTFS and EXT4 also read and write; HFS+ is read-only.
  • Audio in: WAV, AIF/AIFF, FLAC, MP3, OGG.
  • Format a card from the Browser: shift + Format.
